Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · applies to the Android app "LOOPWAVE" (com.loop17.loopwave)

In short

LOOPWAVE collects no personal data. The app has no user account, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not send any data to us or to third parties.

1. Controller

2. Data stored by the app

To keep your settings and best scores, the app stores the following values only locally on your device:

ValuePurpose
LanguageGerman, English or Russian interface
Difficultylast selected level
Sound on/off, volumeaudio settings
Vibration on/offhaptic feedback
Theme (light/dark)appearance
Best score per levelhigh score display

This data never leaves your device and cannot be used to identify you.

3. No internet transmission

All content of the app – including fonts – is bundled with the app. The app does not connect to any server. The "internet access" permission listed by the system is a technical default of the framework used (Capacitor) and is not used by LOOPWAVE.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration – for short haptic feedback during play. Can be turned off in the app's settings.

5. Google Play

When you download LOOPWAVE from Google Play, Google processes data under its own responsibility (e.g. account data, purchase history). If you have agreed to share diagnostic data in your Android settings, Google also provides us with anonymised crash and performance statistics that cannot identify individuals. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The app does not collect any data – including from children.

7. Deleting data

You can delete all stored values at any time by uninstalling the app or via Settings → Apps → LOOPWAVE → Storage → Clear data.

8. Your rights

The app itself does not transmit personal data to us. Under the GDPR you nevertheless have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that involves collecting data, this policy will be updated beforehand.

The statements about local storage and no data transmission concern the app. Visits to this website, emails and payments are covered by the following information:

  • Website: This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs IP addresses for security purposes. We do not embed analytics, external fonts or PayPal widgets. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Email: When you contact us, we process your email address, message and any information you voluntarily provide to handle your enquiry. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information. We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless legal retention requirements apply.
  • PayPal: Support is voluntary. PayPal processes personal data when you open the link or make a payment. We may receive sender and payment details made available to payment recipients. We use them to process the payment and, where applicable, comply with legal retention requirements. More information: PayPal Privacy Statement.

Depending on the activity, the legal bases are Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contract-related enquiries and payment processing, Article 6(1)(f) for other enquiries and secure website operation, and Article 6(1)(c) for legal obligations. Our legitimate interests are responding to your enquiries and providing this information securely. The privacy rights described above also apply to these activities. Contact: info@loop17.de.

3. Отсутствие передачи данных в интернет

Все материалы, включая шрифты, входят в состав приложения. Приложение не подключается к серверам. Системное разрешение на доступ к интернету является технической настройкой используемого фреймворка Capacitor и не используется LOOPWAVE.

Вибрация используется для краткого тактильного отклика во время игры. Её можно отключить в настройках приложения.

5. Google Play

При загрузке приложения через Google Play компания Google самостоятельно обрабатывает данные, например сведения об аккаунте и истории покупок. Если в настройках Android вы разрешили отправку диагностических данных, Google также предоставляет нам обезличенную статистику сбоев и производительности, не позволяющую определить отдельных пользователей. Подробнее: политика конфиденциальности Google.

6. Дети

Приложение не собирает данные, в том числе данные детей.

7. Удаление данных

Вы можете удалить все сохранённые значения, удалив приложение или выбрав в Android: Настройки → Приложения → LOOPWAVE → Память → Очистить данные.

Само приложение не передаёт нам персональные данные. В отношении обработки персональных данных вы имеете предусмотренные GDPR права на доступ, исправление, удаление, ограничение обработки, переносимость данных и возражение, а также право подать жалобу в надзорный орган. Например: орган по защите данных земли Баден-Вюртемберг.

9. Изменения
